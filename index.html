About

Born in Los Angeles to a global family, Makena grew up with admiration for diverse cultures. Her diverse family influenced her passion for art, design, and fashion. Her Opa worked in the interiors of Hollywood and Los Angeles' most iconic properties, and her late uncle, who served as VP of Marketing at Burberry, often crossed boundaries to contribute to their more avant-garde line, Prorsum. Quantitative structure to uphold the beauty of creative work has become the lens through which Makena approaches her work today, thanks to her upbringing.

Passion and excellence led the way for Makena's adolescent success in swimming under Vladi Sapozhkov. In her adult life, she participates in women's bio-optimization ventures and in various forms of artistic expression. Makena continues to paint, write, and design, as well as bring a given director's vision to life in fashion in front of the camera. She applies pre-instilled principles of success to her creative and intellectual fields, while pursuing ventures that create tangible impact. Makena advocates for animals, climate, equity, and women's health, as evidenced by her venture portfolio and philanthropic endeavors.
